Auto routing

Pick Auto in Factory for normal sessions, or the Auto · Orchestrator / Worker / Validator models for Mission roles. The first message of each session is classified by a fast model, and that session then stays on the model chosen for its task type and role. Each role has its own backup.

Balanced routing

Each Auto session uses the model in its pool (the task type's model plus its backups) whose plan is furthest ahead of its weekly pace: the plan with the most quota that would otherwise reset unused goes first, and plans running behind are saved. Sessions are re-checked on your next message every 15 minutes, and sessions that started as "quick" are re-classified once real work arrives. When every plan in the pool is below the threshold and none is ahead of pace, the session goes to your pay-per-token overflow models.
